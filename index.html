Key takeaways

  • Views dipped in August (165K) and recovered in September (196K), the strongest month of the quarter.
  • Most views come from people who don’t follow Luna yet, so reach is strong. Turning those viewers into followers is the opportunity.
  • September’s gain came with more regular posting (8 posts) and no Reels, so the recap data alone can’t show which format drove it.
  • The most recent week looks strong: 79K views, up 26%, with 68% from non-followers.

Audience growth

  • Followers held at about 26K across June to September.
  • Growth was small but positive: +776 in June (from May) and +68 in August (from July). Exact July and September changes need the full numbers from Joy.
  • With 60% or more of views coming from non-followers, converting that reach is the clearest growth lever.
